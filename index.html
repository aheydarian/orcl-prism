Where Virtual Worlds Meet Human Behavior

The Omni-Reality & Cognition Lab builds virtual, augmented and mixed reality simulators, instruments them with eye tracking and physiological sensing, and uses them to design safer streets, healthier buildings and better training for high-stakes work.

About the Lab

We build virtual, augmented and mixed reality simulators with eye tracking and physiological sensing, and pair them with field studies and AI. We use them to test designs before they are built, train people for high-stakes work, and study behavior that is unsafe or impractical to observe in the real world, from streets and bike lanes to buildings and hospital rooms.

ORCL is co-directed by Arsalan Heydarian and Donna Chen and is part of UVA's Link Lab. Its projects are supported by the National Science Foundation, the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative, the Virginia Department of Transportation, the Federal Highway Administration, Leidos, and UVA's Coulter Center for Translational Research and School of Data Science.
